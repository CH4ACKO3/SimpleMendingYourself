# Simple Mending Yourself 1.6 Compatibility List

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Updated: 2026-10-05 · SMY 0.1.2

Repair jobs follow the bench's item and ingredient filters, allowed-pawn list and SMY's HP thresholds.

## Base game and requirements

**RimWorld 1.6 — Compatible**

Pawns with Basics enabled can repair their worn apparel and equipped weapons. Eligible equipment from official DLC and equipment mods follows Simple Mending's repair definitions.

**[Simple Mending](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3657705987) — Required integration**

Uses Simple Mending's benches, filters, repair costs and repair operation, with personal equipment selection and a configurable speed multiplier. Keep repair materials in storage so they contribute to its resource availability check.

**[Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077) — Compatible**

Works alongside the Harmony patches used by the sidearm and combat mods below.

## Sidearms and combat

**[Combat Extended](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2890901044) — Compatible**

Repairs worn apparel and equipped weapons using Simple Mending's material costs and SMY's durability settings. CE continues to manage ammunition, loadouts and carrying capacity.

**[Simple Sidearms](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=927155256) — Integration**

Repairs registered weapons in both equipment and inventory, matching their weapon type and material.

**[Simpler Sidearms](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809933830) — Integration, SMY 0.1.2+**

Repairs equipped weapons and eligible melee or ranged weapons in inventory, including temporarily carried weapons.

**[Rules of Engagement](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809985752) — Compatible for equipped weapons**

Repairs main-hand and equipped off-hand weapons, including repairs that continue after RoE switches the selected item into inventory. Equip a stored sidearm through RoE's weapon controls to add it to the repair candidates.

## Equipment repair-cost patches

These entries use the repair-cost patches supplied by Simple Mending.

**Royalty — Compatible**

Uses the dedicated repair costs for persona weapons, monoswords, plasmaswords, zeushammers, jump packs and broadshield packs. The selected material follows the item's Simple Mending repair definition.

**[Vanilla Factions Expanded - Empire](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2938820380) — Compatible through Simple Mending**

Uses Simple Mending's plasteel repair costs for deserter helmets and deserter armor, with SMY's bench filters and durability thresholds.

**[Rimsenal Faction Pack - Spacer](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3086137468) — Compatible through Simple Mending**

Uses Simple Mending's dedicated steel and plasteel repair costs for the pack's smart weapons, applying the same personal equipment repair workflow.
